## Brand & Style

This design system pairs the systematic clarity, hyper-legible hierarchy, and utilitarian calm of modern Google interfaces with the grounded soul of Indian artisanal craft. The aesthetic is architectural yet inviting: bright, luminous white grounds, precise hairline divisions, and focused bursts of earthborn terracotta. 

Rather than leaning on nostalgic ornaments or heavy skeuomorphism, artisan warmth is introduced through refined restraint—allowing rich product photography, regional typographic scripts, and focused terracotta accents to stand out against crisp neutral canvases. The design evokes absolute reliability, quiet technological empowerment, and effortless utility for artisans, enterprise curators, and global commerce partners alike.

Key visual pillars:
- **Architectural Clarity:** Strict alignment, pure background planes, and deliberate whitespace supersede decorative containers.
- **Pragmatic Utility:** Workflows feel lightweight and direct, using familiar Google Material-inspired spatial patterns.
- **Artisanal Grounding:** Deep terracotta accents anchor user focus, providing a tactile, organic point of contact within an otherwise pristine digital workspace.

## Colors

The color palette centers on high luminance, immaculate surfaces, and surgically applied focal hues. White and cool-warm grey neutrals carry 90% of the UI to ensure products and data remain front and center.

### Primary & Accent
- **Primary Terracotta (`#A8421E`):** Reserved strictly for high-impact actions, key progress indications, and selected states. It carries the rich warmth of fired clay without compromising digital accessibility standards.
- **Terracotta Container (`#FCEEE9`):** An ultra-soft wash used for subtle active indicators, active navigation rails, or highlight backgrounds.
- **Terracotta Hover (`#913617`):** Deepened shade for active button press and hover interactions.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas for application views, modal bodies, and prominent elevated sheets.
- **Surface Dim (`#F8F9FA`):** Secondary canvas, background fill for side navigation rails, toolbars, and canvas workspaces.
- **Surface Container (`#F1F3F4`):** Segmented controls, input surface fills, and low-contrast card backings.
- **Border Subtle (`#E3E3E3`):** Crisp 1px hairline rules providing structural definition without visual weight.
- **Border Strong (`#C4C7C5`):** Form field strokes, active dividers, and interactive outlines.

### Typography Neutrals
- **On-Surface High Contrast (`#1F1F1F`):** Deep charcoal delivering maximum contrast and readability for headlines and primary metrics.
- **On-Surface Medium Contrast (`#444746`):** Secondary metadata, structural labels, and supportive body copy.
- **On-Surface Muted (`#747775`):** Inactive iconography, placeholder text, and subtle timestamps.

### Functional Roles
- **Success / Verified (`#137333`):** Muted forest green inspired by natural leaf dyes, reserved for verified artisan badges, positive financial metrics, and confirmation banners.
- **Success Container (`#E6F4EA`):** Gentle tinted background for success alerts and verified chips.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans drives the typographic hierarchy with crisp geometries, open apertures, and modern balance.

### Multilingual Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Dual-Script Pairing:** For bilingual interfaces (e.g., Hindi primary with secondary English translations), the vernacular script sits at `title-md` or `body-lg` in bold/semi-bold weight, accompanied immediately below by English text scaled 2px smaller at `body-sm` (`#444746`).
- **Scale and Spacing Over Enclosure:** Contrast is achieved by stark differences in scale (e.g., pairing a 32px headline directly with 14px secondary body copy) separated by generous 16px to 24px vertical gaps, rather than bounding text inside tinted background containers.
- **Tabular Numerals:** For inventory counters, pricing matrices, and dimension specs, numerals use fixed-width figures for uniform vertical scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point base grid with 4-point sub-steps for micro-components like input padding and icon margins.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid. 24px gutters, max-width bounded to 1440px with dynamic exterior margins (minimum 64px).
- **Tablet (600px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px page margins.
- **Mobile (0 - 599px):** 4-column grid with 16px gutters and 16px lateral margins.

### Spatial Discipline
- Group connected meta-information tightly with `space-4` and `space-8`.
- Separate autonomous functional sections using `space-32` or `space-48`.
- Canvas cards use interior edge padding of `space-20` on mobile and `space-24` on desktop, preserving breathability around complex artisan catalog items.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids murky or heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows. Depth is established through crisp 1px borders, subtle tonal background tiering, and clean, high-diffusion ambient shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Background `Surface Pure` or `Surface Dim`. Uses 1px borders (`#E3E3E3`) for separation.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Lists):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 1px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.03);` with a 1px border (`#E3E3E3`). Surfaces float just above the canvas for scannable grid arrangements.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Popovers):** `box-shadow: 0 2px 6px 2px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.08), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.04);` with borderless or crisp `#E3E3E3` outline.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Dialogs):** `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px 4px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.12), 0 4px 8px 0 rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.06);`. Paired with a backdrop dim of `rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.32)` to maintain a bright, high-clarity environment.

## Shapes

The geometric language balances utility with modern humanism. Large decorative blob shapes are strictly eliminated in favor of clean 8px to 16px geometry.

### Radii Application
- **Standard (`rounded`, 8px):** Applied to form fields, nested media thumbnails, utility list items, and standard secondary action buttons.
- **Large (`rounded-lg`, 16px):** Applied to primary product cards, interactive preview tiles, dashboards panels, and modular bottom sheets.
- **Extra Large (`rounded-xl`, 24px):** Reserved for modal overlays, contextual banners, and large hero utility modules.
- **Pill (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Applied exclusively to metadata chips, status tags (e.g., "Verified Artisan"), badge counters, and floating bottom navigation triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#A8421E`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 8px (`rounded`), height 40px (desktop) / 44px (mobile), padding `0 24px`. Hover state darkens to `#913617`. No drop shadow; clean and solid.
- **Secondary Outlined:** Background `transparent`, border 1px solid `#C4C7C5`, text `#1F1F1F`, radius 8px. Hover fills with `#F8F9FA`.
- **Tertiary Text:** Text `#A8421E`, background `transparent`, padding `0 12px`. Hover fills with `#FCEEE9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Filter Chips:** Height 32px, `rounded-full` shape. Unselected: background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E3E3E3`, text `#444746`. Selected: background `#FCEEE9`, border 1px solid `#A8421E`, text `#A8421E`.
- **Verified Artisan Tag:** Height 24px, `rounded-full`, background `#E6F4EA`, text `#137333`, border none, icon size 14px with 4px margin.

### Input Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#C4C7C5`, radius 8px, horizontal padding 16px. Typography sits at `body-md` (`#1F1F1F`).
- **Focus State:** 2px solid outline in `#A8421E` with zero ambient halo blur.
- **Labeling:** Floating or top-stacked label in `label-md` (`#444746`), transitioning to `#A8421E` on active focus.

### Cards
- **Product & Artisan Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E3E3E3`, radius 16px (`rounded-lg`), elevation Level 1. Image containers fill top boundary with 1:1 or 4:3 aspect ratios, nested with crisp top 16px radius.
- **Inner Padding:** 16px content body directly below the media divider, keeping metadata clean without visual clutter.

### Lists & Tables
- **Data Rows:** Background `#FFFFFF`, separated by 1px bottom border `#E3E3E3`. Alternating row tints are avoided; scan lines rely on 12px vertical padding.
- **Hover State:** Row background transitions smoothly to `#F8F9FA`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Controls:** 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), border 2px solid `#5C5E61`, radius 4px (checkbox).
- **Checked State:** Fill `#A8421E`, border `#A8421E`, icon/dot in `#FFFFFF`.
