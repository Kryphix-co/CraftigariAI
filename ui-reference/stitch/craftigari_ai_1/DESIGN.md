## Brand & Style

This design system embodies **Modern Indian Craft-Tech**: an intersection of earthy artisan authenticity and clean, utilitarian intelligence. It honors the human spirit of Indian craftsmanship without resorting to visual clichés like decorative mandalas, repetitive paisleys, or kitsch emblems. Instead, it translates the tactile qualities of pottery, woven textiles, and natural materials into a high-utility digital workspace.

### Brand Personality & Core Philosophy
- **Artisan-Centered & Dignified:** Conceived for independent and marginalized Indian creators. The tone is respectful, plainspoken, and empowering—rejecting fake business metrics, tech jargon, and paternalistic UI tropes.
- **Action-First Simplicity:** Eliminates the classic SaaS multi-widget dashboard clutter. The system prioritizes immediate action: capture a product image, speak naturally in regional dialects, and review intelligently extracted listing structures.
- **Organic Intelligence:** Avoids generic AI aesthetics such as purple-neon glows, floating orbs, or sparkle glyphs. Intelligence is communicated through structural clarity, explainable breakdowns, and an expressive visual thread.

### Signature Motif: The Functional Thread
The central metaphor is the continuous artisan thread (`#A84C2A`). It serves as an active visual connector rather than passive ornament:
1. Transforming into an organic audio waveform during voice capture.
2. Weaving unstructured voice notes and raw images into structured catalogue schemas.
3. Visually linking cost factors (materials, labor, complexity) to converge on a justifiable price.
4. Serving as an unbroken progressive step indicator across the publishing workflow.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from raw, sun-baked earth, natural pigments, and unbleached loom fabrics. It establishes high-contrast readability in bright outdoor sunlight while maintaining a tactile, warm presence.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#A84C2A` - Terracotta Clay):** The primary focus color. Reserved for dominant actions ("Add Product", "Publish Listing"), live microphone states, key progression indicators, and the functional thread motif.
- **Secondary (`#2F5233` - Deep Indigo/Forest Earth):** Conveys confirmation, verification badges, authentic craft validation, and positive price/margin stability.
- **Tertiary (`#B87333` - Raw Ochre / Copper):** Used for contextual explanations, educational tooltips, and informational breakdowns.
- **Neutral Surface Ecosystem:**
  - `background-base` (`#F6F1E7`): Natural, unbleached ivory canvas providing an organic foundation.
  - `surface-container` (`#FFFCF6`): Cream-white surface for cards and active touch panels, ensuring crisp contrast without cold blue-white glare.
  - `border-subtle` (`#DDD4C6`): Crisp, soft borders delineating structure without harsh dropshadows.
- **Typography Neutrals:**
  - `text-primary` (`#24211D`): Deep charcoal earth with an accessible 13:1 contrast ratio against the ivory ground.
  - `text-secondary` (`#6E685F`): Muted warm slate for secondary guidance and instructional context.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes legibility, generous line height, and clarity across diverse device sizes. It relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for both display headlines and body mechanics. The geometric warmth and open apertures of Plus Jakarta Sans ensure effortless readability for artisans interacting with Roman scripts and trans-literated vernacular content.

### Hierarchy & Usage Instructions
- **Display & Section Titles (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Reserved for core action headings ("Add New Craft", "Review Catalogue") and transition checkpoints. Keep statements short, active, and reassuring.
- **Content & Body Text (`body-lg`, `body-md`):** Uses an open `lineHeight` multiplier (1.5–1.55x) to prevent ocular fatigue. Small sizes under 13px are strictly forbidden in core artisan workflows to accommodate lower literacy levels.
- **Micro-Copy & Explanations (`label-sm`, `body-sm`):** Avoid low-contrast or fine-weight letters. All metadata, prices, and attribute chips use clear weights (`500` or `600`) to guarantee high daylight contrast.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial strategy shifts from a tactile, thumbs-first single column on mobile to a deliberate, split-canvas comparison view on desktop.

### Mobile Grid & Ergonomics
- **Base Rhythm:** Built on an 8px base grid system (`space-xs` through `space-3xl`).
- **Touch Targets:** The primary interaction targets (Camera capture, Mic triggers, Continue steps) enforce a minimum height of `48px` (`3rem`), with `56px` to `64px` preferred for thumb reachability.
- **Stacking:** Mobile views use single-column task flows with bottom-anchored actions to enable one-handed operation while holding a craft piece.

### Desktop Split-Architecture
- **Dual-Pane Transformation:** Instead of stretching mobile fields into expansive empty spaces, desktop view utilizes a split 12-column grid:
  - **Left Pane (5 Columns):** Sticky raw assets (captured high-res artisan photography, live audio recording playback, raw transcription notes).
  - **Right Pane (7 Columns):** Live-woven market listing preview, generated multi-lingual catalogue fields, and explainable pricing breakdown models.
- **Maximum Width:** Workspaces are capped at `1200px` (`container-max-w`) to maintain direct ocular scanning connections between inputs and outputs.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation avoids heavy artificial drop shadows and glossy tech blurs. Instead, spatial hierarchy relies on **tactile tonal layering** and **low-contrast clay-dusted outlines**.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#F6F1E7` (Natural Ivory). Unelevated background for structural canvas areas.
- **Active Floor (Cards & Action Surfaces):** `#FFFCF6` (Pure Warm Cream). Rests 1 level up with a crisp `1px solid #DDD4C6` border. This border-based definition separates surfaces without heavy shadows.
- **Floating Controls (Active Bar & Mic Pill):** Elevated with an ambient, clay-tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(168, 76, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 33, 29, 0.05)`.
- **Focused State:** Elements receiving active attention (recording node, selected pricing factor) illuminate their perimeter with a `2px solid #A84C2A` halo rather than an artificial glowing effect.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** profile. It delivers a hand-finished, organic edge reminiscent of polished wood and wet clay, avoiding harsh corners as well as overly playful circular containers.

### Radii Hierarchy
- **Base Components (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form fields, input boxes, craft detail chips, and contextual tooltips.
- **Interactive Surfaces & Cards (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Primary container blocks, smart catalogue panels, and product raw-to-market split cards.
- **Signature Interactive Elements (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Product photo previews, camera viewport windows, and modular workflow segments.
- **Complete Pills (`rounded-full`):** Audio waveform tracker, state badges (e.g., "Draft", "AI Weaving", "Published"), and the primary floating mic trigger.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Add Product", "Publish Listing"):**
  - Background: `#A84C2A` (Terracotta).
  - Text: `#FFFFFF` (High contrast, bold 600).
  - Height: `52px` minimum on mobile. Roundedness: `8px`.
  - State: Active press scales slightly to `98%` with zero drop shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost (e.g., "Retake Photo", "Adjust Manually"):**
  - Background: `#FFFCF6` with border `1px solid #DDD4C6`.
  - Text: `#24211D`.
- **Audio Capture Trigger:**
  - Round `64px` floating center module.
  - Background: `#A84C2A` with gentle pulse rings in `#F4EAE4` during active recording.

### 2. Functional Thread & Audio Waveform
- The continuous thread renders at `2px` stroke weight in `#A84C2A`.
- **Voice State:** Transforms into a responsive sine wave tracking voice amplitudes without mechanical digital frequency bars.
- **AI Weaving State:** Connects extracted keywords (e.g., "Terracotta" → "Hand-carved" → "Rajasthan") via a curved SVG thread that turns solid green (`#2F5233`) upon verification.

### 3. Smart Pricing Convergence Guide
- Clean vertical factor stack showing:
  - Material Investment + Time & Labor + Craft Rarity.
- Shows factors converging through the continuous thread directly into the **Recommended Price Display** (`headline-xl`).
- Clear plain-language explanation: "Why this price?" with zero ambiguous probability metrics or fake confidence percentages.

### 4. Form Fields & Editable Chips
- **Inputs:** Minimal border-based styling (`1px solid #DDD4C6`), background `#FFFCF6`, large `16px` typography to prevent iOS zoom-in.
- **Craft Attribute Chips:** Removable, editable tags with a tactile cream ground (`#EFE8DC`), dark charcoal label, and an accessible tap area.

### 5. Cards & Containers
- Cards are restricted to 1 level of hierarchy to prevent nesting traps.
- Surfaces use `#FFFCF6` framed by `#DDD4C6`. Internal dividers use a subtle `0.5px` border or whitespace separation.
